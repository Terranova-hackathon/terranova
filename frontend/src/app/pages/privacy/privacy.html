Our Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data LunaNova collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

Who Is Responsible for Your Data

The data controller for this website is LunaNovaGames.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at gdpr-request@lunanova.games.

What Information We Collect

When you browse without an account

You can search and browse games without creating an account. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not set any cookies before you log in. However:

  • Like any website, our server receives technical data with each request (your IP address, browser type and the page requested). This is used only to deliver the page and keep the service secure.
  • Game cover images and screenshots are loaded directly from IGDB's image servers (images.igdb.com). Your browser therefore connects to IGDB, which receives your IP address and browser information. See Third-Party Services below.

When you create an account

Account details
Your email address, an optional display name, and your password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form.
Account records
The date your account was created and the date you accepted this Privacy Policy.
Favorites
The games you save to your favorites list, and when you saved them.

An email address and password are required to create an account. If you do not want to provide them, you can still browse and search games without one.

How We Use Your Information and Why

Under the GDPR we must have a legal basis for each use of your data:

Running your account
Creating your account, logging you in and storing your favorites. Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
Security and delivery
Processing technical request data to serve the site and protect it against abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
Policy acceptance
Recording when you accepted this policy, so we can show which version applied to you. Legal basis: our legal obligation to demonstrate compliance (Art. 6(1)(c) and Art. 5(2) GDPR).

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or make any automated decisions about you.

Cookies and Local Storage

We only store what is strictly necessary for features you use:

LunaNova_auth (cookie)
Keeps you logged in. Set only when you log in, removed when you log out, and expires automatically after 24 hours.
theme (local storage)
Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

How Long We Keep Your Data

Your account data and favorites are kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from your account page, which permanently removes your account and favorites.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information, including hashed passwords, an HttpOnly login cookie that scripts cannot read, and a database that is not publicly accessible. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

We share data only with the following services, and only as described:

IGDB (Twitch Interactive, Inc.)
Provides our game information and images. Game searches are sent from our server, without any information about you. Images are loaded by your browser directly from IGDB, so IGDB receives your IP address. Twitch is based in the United States. See the Twitch Privacy Notice.
Price data (GG.deals, Steam)
Our server requests game prices from these services. No information about you is sent.
Hosting
Our servers and database are hosted by DigitalOcean in Frankfurt, Germany.

Your Rights

Under the EU's GDPR, you have the following rights:

Right to Access
You can ask what personal data we hold about you and how we process it.
Right to Rectification
You can ask us to correct or complete your personal data. You can also change your display name yourself on your account page.
Right to Erasure
You can ask us to delete your personal data, or delete your account yourself on your account page.
Right to Restriction
You can ask us to stop processing your data without deleting it, for example while a correction is checked.
Right to Object
You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
Data Portability
You can ask to receive your account data and favorites in a machine-readable format, or have them sent to another provider.
Right to Complain
You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Data Requests

We respond to all requests within one month, as required by the GDPR. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, usually by replying from the email address registered to your account.

To make a data request:

  1. Email us at gdpr-request@lunanova.games
  2. Include in the subject: [GDPR] (your request)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last Updated" date. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will also tell you when you next log in.
