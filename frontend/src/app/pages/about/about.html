About LunaNova

Find your next game, keep track of prices and decide what and when to buy.

What is LunaNova?

LunaNova is a game discovery site. Anyone can search thousands of games, filter by genre, platform, developer or publisher, and compare prices across stores. Create a free account to save games to your favorites.

Game information comes from IGDB and prices from GG.deals and Steam. LunaNova is open source and was built during the Terranova hackathon with ASP.NET Core and Angular.
